# Chart Honesty Checklist
For every chart, mark Yes / No / Not applicable and explain any No:
1. Is the original source official and traceable?
2. Is the dataset title and reference year shown?
3. Are geography and population coverage clear?
4. Are units and denominators labelled?
5. Is the chart title descriptive rather than sensational?
6. Does a bar chart use a zero baseline?
7. Are axes, ticks, scales and rounding clearly labelled?
8. Are category definitions consistent across comparisons?
9. Are missing values shown as missing rather than zero?
10. Are time periods comparable in definition and coverage?
11. Does the takeaway distinguish observation from explanation?
12. Is correlation kept separate from causation?
13. Are data limitations placed near the chart?
14. Are colours legible, accessible, and not the only way to convey meaning?
15. Is the chart understandable to a non-specialist reader?
16. Has a human checked every number against the source?
