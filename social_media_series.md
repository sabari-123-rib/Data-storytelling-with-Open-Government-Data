# Five-post social media series

## Post 1

📊 India’s school system is vast: UDISE+ reports 1,471,891 schools in 2023–24. That number describes scale—not automatically quality, attendance, or learning. Read the data with context. Source: Ministry of Education, UDISE+ 2023–24.

## Post 2

📈 UDISE+ 2023–24 reports gross enrolment ratios of 93.0% at primary level and 56.2% at higher-secondary level. GER is not an attendance or completion rate, and the figures alone do not explain why the difference exists. Source: Ministry of Education.

## Post 3

🏫 School-stage enrolment counts are not a student-tracking chart. Differences between primary and secondary totals may reflect several factors, so we should not call them ‘dropouts’ without additional evidence. Source: UDISE+ 2023–24.

## Post 4

👧👦 UDISE+ reports 113.25 million girls and 121.71 million boys enrolled from primary to higher secondary in 2023–24. Counts are useful, but fair comparisons also need population denominators and context. Source: Ministry of Education.

## Post 5

🤖 AI can help explain charts and automate updates—but it can also invent explanations. Verify every claim against the original dataset, show the year, cite the source, and disclose limitations. Honest data builds trust.
